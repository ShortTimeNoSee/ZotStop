# Changelog

## [1.0.0](https://github.com/ShortTimeNoSee/ZotStop/releases/tag/v1.0.0)

Initial release.

- Web/PWA and signed Android app with live operator positions and available arrival estimates.
- Route comparison, shared-stop arrivals, saved stops, watched stops, and named route and stop views.
- Interactive campus map with directional routes, progressive road and building detail, parks, water, and offline data.
- Nearby departures, stop and line search, recent stops, and links that reopen a stop.
- Timed stops from the operator feed. Other stops can be passed when nobody is waiting and nobody on board requested the stop.
- Boarding guidance and links to official schedules.
- Optional one-time location and foreground tracking, with native Android and iOS location integrations.
- Get-off alerts that follow this phone or a bus selected by the fleet name printed on it. A phone alert stays on the device. A bus alert uses the operator's position and does not guess a vehicle.
- Optional approximate rider hints and randomized usage counts.
- Daily retention cleanup for aggregate usage counts.
- Production web tests and Android and iOS simulator flows in CI.
- Installation, development, privacy, contribution, and security documentation.
